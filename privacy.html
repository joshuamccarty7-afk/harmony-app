HARMONY privacy policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

HARMONY is a private application used by one person, its owner, on the owner's own computer. It is not available to anyone else.

What HARMONY accesses

  • Gmail (read-only): only messages the owner has labeled for HARMONY or that come from senders the owner has approved.
  • Google Calendar (read-only): upcoming events on the owner's calendars.

HARMONY cannot send, delete, or change email, and cannot create or change calendar events.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to show the owner their own schedule and commitments.
  • Data is processed and stored on the owner's own computer. Access credentials are kept in the computer's secure keychain.
  • Data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone else.
  • HARMONY's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect HARMONY at any time in HARMONY's settings or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting also deletes the synced data from the computer.
